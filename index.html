Why we're here

The Goal.

Own the loudest week in New York.

For two weeks the whole world is watching tennis, and it's happening in NY. We're dropping two new toys — ACE and SMASH — right into the middle of it.

We're not here to make nice content. We're here to be impossible to miss.

Target
10–15M views.

Across the four days. Nothing gets us there but volume and nerve. Post like it.

Everything is tennis.

Hot & Cold — tennis extras not in the deck

  • Grunting on court?
  • A man who can cook vs. a man who can serve?
  • Sharing your live location during a match?
  • Wearing his player's shirt to the Open?

Red Flag / Green Flag

  • He owns more racquets than plates.
  • She brings her own snacks to the stadium.
  • Says “we” about their favourite player.
  • Won't let you win. Ever.
  • Splits the bill on a first date.

Love means zero

  • In tennis, love means zero — what does love mean to you?
  • What's your score right now: 40–love, or deuce?
  • What would you go to deuce over?

Ace or Fault

  • Sliding into the DMs mid-match.
  • A first date at the Open.
  • Texting your ex during a rain delay.
  • Bringing a date to meet your friends on day one.

Smash or Pass — New York edition

  • The subway at rush hour.
  • A $9 iced coffee.
  • Times Square on a Saturday.
  • Dating apps in this city.
  • The honey deuce.

Say It Louder

  • What's something you wish women said louder?
  • What's the most New York way to get dumped?
  • Describe your type in two words.
